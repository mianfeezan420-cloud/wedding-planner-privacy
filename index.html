Privacy Policy — Wedding Planner

Developer: F-Tech Developers
Effective date: 13 November 2026

Wedding Planner is an offline-first Pakistani wedding planning app (checklists, budget, guests, vendors and cultural planning tools). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. Your planning data stays on your device

Everything you enter in the app — couple names, wedding and event dates, checklist tasks, budget and expenses, guest lists, vendor details, and entries in the planning tools (such as Salami register, outfits, Nikah details and timelines) — is stored only on your own device in the app's local storage. The app has no user accounts and no server of ours: we do not receive, collect, sell or have access to your planning data.

If you use the app's Backup / Export features, the backup file is created only when you tap export, is saved or shared only where you choose (for example your own storage or an app you pick in the share sheet), and is entirely under your control. Restoring a backup reads only the file you select.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows a banner advertisement at the bottom of the screen, provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, device information, general (approximate) location, and ad interaction data, in line with Google's own policies. You can learn more at policies.google.com/privacy and control ad personalisation in your device's Google settings (including resetting or deleting your advertising ID).

Except for this advertising data handled by Google AdMob, the app does not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services, and does not share any data with third parties.

3. Permissions and optional features

  • Contacts (optional): only if you choose to import guests from your phone contacts and grant permission. Imported names/numbers are saved only in the app's local guest list on your device. If you deny permission, the rest of the app works normally.
  • Notifications (optional): used only for local reminders you set, such as task due dates and vendor payment reminders. These reminders are generated on your device.
  • Photos (optional): only if you attach a receipt photo to an expense or pick an image; it is stored locally with that entry.
  • WhatsApp / phone sharing: share buttons (for example sending a task or an invitation) open WhatsApp or your dialer only when you tap them, with content you choose. Nothing is sent automatically.
  • Internet: used to load advertisements from Google AdMob. Your planning features work offline.

4. Data retention and deletion

Your planning data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries inside the app, use "Reset All Local Data" in Settings to erase all app data, or uninstall the app, which removes its locally stored data. Backup files you exported remain wherever you saved them until you delete them yourself.

5. Children

Wedding Planner is a planning tool intended for adults organising a wedding. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
